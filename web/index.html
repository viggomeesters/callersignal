    The evidence protocol

    Unknown is a useful answer.

    CallerSignal separates number-plan facts from identity, lookup demand, and call reports. Missing evidence never becomes a “safe” badge.

    1. NormalizeRecord the input mode, origin decision, and canonical form.
    2. CheckRoute to declared public sources for the numbering jurisdiction.
    3. ExplainReturn evidence, gaps, confidence, freshness, and spoofing risk together.
